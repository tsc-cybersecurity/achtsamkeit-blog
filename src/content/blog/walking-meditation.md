You do not need to sit still to meditate. Walking meditation is one of the oldest contemplative practices, bringing awareness to something we do every day without thinking.

## How to Practice

1. **Choose a path** — even 10 steps of clear space is enough
2. **Stand still** for a moment, feeling your feet on the ground
3. **Begin walking slowly**, noticing each phase: lifting, moving, placing
4. **When your mind wanders**, gently return attention to the sensation of walking
5. **At the end of your path**, pause, turn mindfully, and walk back

## Why Walking Meditation?

For many people, sitting meditation can feel challenging at first. Walking offers a gentle alternative that still cultivates deep presence. It bridges the gap between formal practice and everyday mindfulness.

The rhythm of walking naturally calms the nervous system. Combined with deliberate attention, it becomes a moving meditation that you can practice anywhere — in a park, a hallway, or even your living room.
